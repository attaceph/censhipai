[ cen.ship.ai ] Censorship AI for Enterprise
=========================================


-------------------------|_|-------------
       [ cen.ship.ai ]'s Problem
-----------------------------------------

Businesses need to use AI to automatically generate content
for marketing and brand promotion. However, each country has
its own customs and traditions, and each business has its own
brand strategy. Therefore, reviewing the AI-generated content
to ensure it aligns with the customs and traditions of each
client country and with the business's own brand strategy is
a pressing need. The biggest challenges are simplicity and
affordability.
